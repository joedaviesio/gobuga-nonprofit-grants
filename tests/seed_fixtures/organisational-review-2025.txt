Organisational Review — Kiwi Community Trust
Conducted by: Independent Governance Advisory, March 2025

Executive Summary
Kiwi Community Trust is a well-managed charitable organisation with strong community relationships and growing impact. This review identifies areas for improvement in financial sustainability, governance practices, and operational efficiency.

Strengths
- Deeply trusted by target communities across Northland and BOP
- Strong volunteer base of 120+ regular volunteers
- Effective partnerships with local councils and iwi
- High programme completion rates (87%)
- Experienced leadership team with sector expertise

Areas for Improvement
- Revenue concentration: 55% from government grants
- Board composition lacks financial expertise
- M&E framework needs strengthening
- IT systems require modernisation
- Staff retention below sector average

Recommendations
1. Diversify funding — target 40% government, 30% philanthropy, 20% earned income, 10% donations by 2027
2. Recruit board member with CFO/accounting background
3. Implement outcomes measurement framework (Theory of Change)
4. Invest in cloud-based CRM and programme management tools
5. Develop staff professional development programme
